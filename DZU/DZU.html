A solo project by MoronSky, a developer on the Super Mario Epic Wii (SMEW) team, Timpaniii Team's flagship mod.

New Super Mario Bros. U, reworked to hit harder — and stop playing fair.

What it's about

Danger Zone U starts from New Super Mario Bros. U and keeps the same bones, but pushes everything else further: levels go harder, throw more surprises, and don't let you coast through. It's a solo project, built at its own pace by a member of the SMEW team — Timpaniii Team has no hand in making it.
